🎵 MASTER ACOUSTIC TIP: UNPLUG THE CENTER DASH SPEAKER

While the top rubber tray is lifted off, remove the two 7mm screws holding the center speaker grille and unplug the 2-pin connector from the 3.5" center speaker. Aftermarket Android harnesses downmix stereo into a loud, unpadded mono sum across the center dash, causing harsh vocal clipping and destroying stereo imaging. Unplugging it instantly delivers a wide, crisp concert soundstage from Brandon's front door speakers.

  1. Top Tray Mat: Pry up the rubber liner in the center dash tray.
  2. Tray Bolts: Remove the two 7mm hex screws beneath the rubber mat.
  3. Speaker Grille: Pry up the center speaker / storage assembly; disconnect speaker plug.
  4. Upper Bezel Screws: Remove the two 7mm screws at the top of the radio bezel.
  5. Bezel Pull: Grasp both sides of the main bezel near the bottom AC controls and pull straight back to disengage spring clips. Unplug hazard switch and AC harnesses.
  6. Display & ACM: Remove the four 7mm screws holding the factory screen assembly and remove it from the dash. Loosen or remove the four 7mm screws on the lower CD player/ACM unit only as needed to tilt it forward to plug in the T-harness and route wiring into the lower cavern, then bolt the ACM securely back into place.

  • CANbus Decoder Box: Plug the multi-pin CAN connector firmly into the black CANbox (Raise / Hiworld). Wrap in fleece tape to prevent dash rattles.

📲 1. STEP-BY-STEP WIRELESS CARPLAY & ANDROID AUTO SETUP
  1. CRITICAL FIRST STEP — FORGET OLD FORD SYNC: On Brandon's iPhone (or Android), open Settings > Bluetooth, locate SYNC or Ford F-150, tap (i) and select "Forget This Device". If your phone tries to connect to the old truck Bluetooth and the new radio at the same time, CarPlay will disconnect or freeze.
  2. Open Zlink / SpeedPlay: On the Incifum 12.1" radio screen, find and open the pre-installed app named Zlink (or SpeedPlay / AutoKit). Keep it open on the screen waiting for connection.
  3. Pair via Bluetooth: On your phone, scan for Bluetooth devices. Tap the radio's Bluetooth name (typically BT-CAR, CarKit, or Android-BT).
  4. Enter PIN & Approve: Enter PIN 0000 or 1234. When your iPhone prompts "Use CarPlay with 'BT-CAR'?", tap USE CARPLAY. (On Android, allow Android Auto permissions).
  5. Hands Off the Wi-Fi: The radio will automatically establish an ad-hoc Wi-Fi connection in the background for high-speed video/audio streaming. Do not manually disconnect from the radio's Wi-Fi network in your phone settings!
🎛️ 2. HOW TO ACTIVATE STEERING WHEEL CONTROLS (CANBUS PROTOCOL SETUP)

If Volume Up/Down, Track Skip, or Mute on the steering wheel do not respond immediately, the internal CANbus protocol profile simply needs to be set to the Ford F-150 profile:

  1. On the radio, open Settings > Car Settings > Factory Settings (or Factory Setup).
  2. When prompted for the password, enter 8888 (or backup PINs: 3368, 1234, or 1617) and tap OK.
  3. Tap CANbus / Protocol Settings (or Car Model Selection).
  4. Select Protocol Box Brand: Look at the black CANbus decoder box on your harness and select the matching brand name printed on its sticker (typically Raise, Hiworld, or Simple Soft).
  5. Select Make: Ford
  6. Select Model: F-150 (or F150)
  7. Select Year/Trim: 2015-2018 → Choose Base/Low (if replacing 4.2" screen) or High (if replacing 8" screen).
  8. Tap Save / Confirm. The unit will reboot. Once restarted, all steering wheel buttons talk directly to the Ford network!
  9. Optional fine-tuning: If any specific steering wheel button needs custom mapping, open the Steering Learn / SWC app from the main app drawer, tap the on-screen function, and hold the steering wheel button for 2 seconds.
🖼️ 3. HOW TO SET A CUSTOM WALLPAPER / BACKGROUND PICTURE

Customize the 12.1" portrait display with Brandon's favorite wallpaper (Hulk artwork, Ford emblem, or custom background):

  1. Method 1 (Long-Press Home Screen): Press and hold your finger on any empty area of the Android home screen for 2 seconds. A menu will pop up reading "Wallpapers" or "Themes". Tap Wallpapers.
  2. Method 2 (From Flash Drive / USB): Save any high-res picture (1024x768 or 1920x1080 .JPG or .PNG) onto a standard USB flash drive. Plug the drive into the radio's USB cable. Open the Gallery or File Manager app, tap the photo, tap the Menu (3 dots) at the top right, and select "Set as Wallpaper".
  3. Method 3 (System Settings): Go to Settings > Display > Wallpaper, browse the built-in dynamic dark themes or your photo gallery, adjust the crop, and tap Apply to Home Screen.

🔋 BATTERY GUARD Reddit r/f150 & XDA

CANbus Sleep & Standby Battery Drain

The Trap: If the radio is set to "Instant Fast Boot / Never Sleep", certain CANbus decoders continue to ping the truck's MS-CAN network after the key is removed, drawing 150mA–300mA and flattening the truck battery after a 3-day weekend.

⚡ PRO CRAFTSMAN FIX:

Open Factory Settings (PIN 126 or 3368) → Power / Standby Setting. Configure Auto Sleep Timeout to 24 Hours or Full Cold Shutdown. When Brandon leaves the truck parked for days, the unit cleanly powers off completely, preserving 100% of the Ford AGM battery.

📷 BACKUP CAM YouTube Stereo Fabricators

Brake-Pedal Camera Static & Ground Loops

The Trap: Ford third brake lights and tail lights pulse with high-frequency PWM voltage. If the yellow camera RCA barrel touches the bare steel dash framework, pressing the brake pedal causes the screen to flicker with horizontal lines or momentarily black out.

⚡ PRO CRAFTSMAN FIX:

Wrap the yellow female-to-male RCA connection in 3 tight layers of 3M electrical tape or slide heat shrink tubing over it. Isolating the RCA metal barrel from chassis metal completely eliminates brake-induced video interference.

🔥 DASH FABRICATION F150Forum Teardowns

Brittle Sun-Baked AC Vent & Hazard Clips

The Trap: 2018 F-150 plastic louvers endure heavy sun baking through the windshield. Cold prying on the OEM hazard/traction switch or vent retaining clips snaps them instantly, making buttons sink into the new bezel.

⚡ PRO CRAFTSMAN FIX:

Warm the backside retaining tabs with a hair dryer on warm for 40 seconds before prying. The softened plastic flexes smoothly without cracking, locking tight into the new 12.1" bezel with factory-firm tactile clicks.

Issue: AC/Heater Not Blowing, Stuck in Celsius, or Defrost Only

Cause: Missing secondary 6-pin climate sub-harness, interrupted actuator calibration sweep, or CANbus profile mismatch.

Brandon's Step-by-Step Fix:
  1. Verify the 6-pin white multi-wire plug running from the lower physical button cluster is plugged directly into the radio's secondary climate port.
  2. Go to Settings → Car Settings → Factory Settings (Enter PIN: 8888).
  3. Select Canbus Settings → Select your CANbox brand (Raise or Hiworld) → Ford → F-150 → 2015-2018.
  4. Select Manual AC if your truck had 3 rotary dials, or Auto AC if it had digital dual-zone displays.
  5. Toggle Temperature Unit from °C to °F.
  6. Tap Save & Reboot. Then turn key to ON (engine OFF) and wait 60 seconds silently for blend doors to calibrate.

Issue: Reverse Camera Black Screen / "No Signal" or Double Lines

Cause: Video standard mismatch (default is AHD 720p, but Ford OEM is NTSC 480i) or voltage missing (OEM camera requires 6V DC).

Brandon's Step-by-Step Fix:
  1. Enter Factory Settings (PIN: 8888) → Reverse / Backcar Settings.
  2. Change camera video resolution from AHD 720P to NTSC or CVBS.
  3. Toggle "Parking Guidelines: OFF" so only the Ford factory dynamic lines display.
  4. Ensure the yellow RCA plug is connected to CAM-VIN, NOT AUX-IN or VIDEO-IN.
  5. Measure voltage on camera power lead: verify it outputs +6.0V DC when shifted into Reverse.

Issue: Total Silence on 2018 B&O Play Audio System

Cause: 2018 B&O systems do not accept analog speaker signals; audio is carried digitally over an A2B bus from the factory ACM.

Brandon's Step-by-Step Fix:
  • If equipped with B&O, keep factory ACM CD player plugged into its harness behind lower dash cavity to drive the A2B bus.
  • In Factory Settings (8888) → Canbus Setup → select Ford -> F-150 -> 2015-2018 -> With Amp / B&O so the CANbox sends the digital wake-up packet.
  • For non-B&O base trucks: Ensure the Blue/White AMP-CON wire is connected to trigger the factory audio relay.

Issue: Severe Echo & Tunnel Sound During Hands-Free Phone Calls

Cause: Internal front-panel microphone preamp gain is set too high (15 default), causing cabin acoustic feedback.

Brandon's Step-by-Step Fix:
  1. Non-destructive first: Enter Factory Settings (8888) → Audio/Voice Settings → reduce Mic Gain from 15 down to 6 - 8.
  2. Plug in the provided 3.5mm external lapel mic and route it to your sun visor / A-pillar.
  3. Recommended Microphone Setup: Plug the included external 3.5mm lapel microphone into the rear MIC-IN port and route it to the driver-side sun visor or A-pillar. In Android sound settings, set Mic Input to External Mic Priority and adjust Mic Gain to 6–8 for crystal-clear calls with zero echo.

Issue: Parasitic Battery Drain (Dead Battery After 2-3 Days)

Cause: "Auto Sleep" (Fast Boot) mode keeps the CPU in suspend mode and the CANbus box continuously pings the Ford Gateway Module.

Brandon's Step-by-Step Fix:
  1. Open Android Settings → Car Settings → System Settings.
  2. Find Shutdown Delay (or Sleep Mode).
  3. Change value from "3 Days / Auto Sleep" to 0 Minutes / Power Off Immediately.
  4. The unit will perform a clean shutdown when the key is removed and driver's door opens, dropping standby drain below 15 mA.

Issue: CarPlay / Android Auto Disconnects or Lag

Cause: 2.4GHz Wi-Fi congestion or phone trying to connect to the old Ford Sync Bluetooth simultaneously.

Brandon's Step-by-Step Fix:
  1. On your iPhone, go to Bluetooth settings and FORGET "SYNC" completely.
  2. In Android radio settings, set the Wi-Fi hotspot band to 5.0 GHz (instead of 2.4 GHz).
  3. In Zlink settings, set Driver Position to Left, and connection mode to Auto.

Issue: Passenger Airbag Warning Light / SRS Fault (DTC B1202 / B00D5)

Cause: Upper hazard switch strip containing Passenger Airbag Deactivation indicator was unplugged while vehicle battery was connected.

Brandon's Step-by-Step Fix:
  1. Always disconnect the negative battery terminal before unplugging the dash.
  2. Ensure the factory airbag indicator module is snapped securely into the new bezel and its plug clicked in firmly before reconnecting the battery.
  3. If light is already on: Disconnecting the battery will NOT clear SRS codes. Connect an OBDLink EX adapter, open FORScan on a laptop, access the RCM module, and click Clear DTCs.

Issue: Center Console Factory USB Hub Dead

Cause: Illuminated dual USB ports in center console plug into OEM Sync APIM via proprietary 4-pin mini-USB cable.

Issue: Screen Goes 100% Pitch Black When Headlights Turn On

Cause: Default Topway firmware sets the night backlight slider to 0%.

Brandon's Step-by-Step Fix:
  1. Turn headlight switch OFF so screen returns to day mode.
  2. Go to Settings → Display (or Car Settings → Display Settings).
  3. Set Day Backlight to 85% - 90%.
  4. Set Night Backlight to 40% - 45% (NEVER set below 30%).
  5. In Factory Settings (8888), verify Dimmer Mode is set to CANbus (Auto).
